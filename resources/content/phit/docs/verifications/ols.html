phit’s fit_ols_matrix() is compared with R’s lm() and statsmodels’ OLS on the same observations. The verification notebook shows the calls and results side by side, and the math notebook derives the calculations. Shared metrics and tolerance conventions are described in Verification.

Compared

Coefficients, covariance, standard errors, t statistics, p-values, 95% confidence intervals, fitted values, residuals, R-squared, and log-likelihood. The models use an intercept, homoskedastic covariance, residual variance RSS/(n − p), Student’s t tests, and ML variance RSS/n for the likelihood.

The housing rows in docs/data/housing.csv are simulated; the math notebook describes how they were generated. The other cases come from tests/fixtures/ols-r.json.

Reference and versions

R 4.6.1 lm(), statsmodels 0.15.0 with NumPy 2.5.4 on Python 3.14, and phit on PHP 8.5.10, all on the development Mac. Linux and other PHP builds are not covered by this report.

The runner gives every implementation the same explicit design. R receives it as y ~ . - 1 and computes R-squared with the same ones-column convention as phit, which avoids a duplicate intercept.

Metric and tolerance

Every field, relative to R: absolute 1e-9 plus relative 1e-7.

Results

All fields in all seven cases pass. ols-results.json holds the inputs, versions, sample metadata, PHP warnings, and differences from R.

In the missing-data case, positions 1 and 3 are dropped and 0, 2, 4, and 5 are retained. phit emits one E_WARNING with the dropped and original counts. The runner checks the positions and counts and records the warning.

Known exceptions

  • Perfect and zero fits, and invalid or singular inputs, are covered by the Rust and PHP tests, not this comparison. Tiny decomposition-dependent residuals make perfect-fit t, p, and likelihood comparisons unstable.
  • R and statsmodels may accept a singular design by aliasing coefficients. phit rejects it.

Reproduction

After the notebook setup:

.context/notebooks-venv/bin/python docs/verifications/compare_ols.py

The script refits every case with Rscript, statsmodels, and PHP, checks every field, and rewrites ols-results.json. cargo test and tests/ols.php run without R or Python.
