phit’s fit_poisson_matrix() is compared with base R glm() and statsmodels GLM with the Poisson family on identical observations. The executed comparison shows the calls and results side by side. Shared metrics are described in Verification.

Compared

Coefficients, covariance, standard errors, normal z tests and p-values, Wald confidence limits, fitted mean counts and response residuals, log-likelihood, deviance, null deviance, AIC, and residual df.

Inputs and scope

Six saved R fixtures: the simulated clinic sample (the multiple-predictor case), a single predictor, no intercept, intercept only, correlated full-rank columns, and sparse counts. The three-way comparison adds a listwise-deletion case with two dropped rows.

Reference and versions

R 4.6.1, statsmodels 0.15.0 with NumPy 2.5.4 on Python 3.14.7, and phit on PHP 8.5.10, on the development Mac. Linux and PHP-FPM are not covered by this report.

  • R intervals use confint.default(fit) (Wald), not profile-likelihood confint(fit).
  • R’s fitting tolerance is 1e-16, so the covariance evaluated at the preceding IRLS iterate is negligible.
  • Without a ones column, the null baseline is mean one. The Python runner computes this convention explicitly.

Metric and tolerance

Absolute 1e-9 plus relative 1e-7 of the R value for every numerical field. Sample accounting must match exactly. Iteration counts may differ.

Results

All fields in all seven cases pass. The saved report records versions, complete outputs, maximum differences from R, warnings, and retained-row metadata.

Known exceptions and limits

The Rust and PHP tests reject insufficient observations, invalid shapes, nonfinite values, negative or fractional outcomes, all-zero samples, dependent columns, and selected boundary designs. They also check scaled predictor units, zero-count and near-fit deviance arithmetic, iteration exhaustion, and missing-row warnings and alignment.

  • No exhaustive test for the existence of a finite MLE is offered.
  • Overdispersion and sparse counts can invalidate conventional Wald inference.
  • These cases use no offset. Offset and exposure fits are checked separately in tests/intercept.php and against R fixtures; see Verification.
