Each walkthrough derives one estimator step by step on a small data set: the objective, the matrices, the solution, the uncertainty, and, where it applies, the likelihood. The pages show saved outputs, so reading them needs no Python or R. Agreement with R and the other references is reported on the Verification pages, and speed on the Benchmarks pages.
