Functions

The Phiz\ namespace provides:

  • Construction: aes, ggplot, field, factor, calc, and expr.
  • Every geom as geom_*, plus stat_ecdf, stat_qq, and stat_qq_line.
  • Scales: scale_x_continuous, scale_x_log10, scale_x_sqrt, scale_x_reverse, scale_x_date, and their y versions, plus scale_color, scale_fill, scale_*_manual, scale_*_scheme, scale_*_viridis, scale_*_brewer, scale_*_gradient, scale_*_gradient2, scale_size, scale_shape, scale_alpha, and scale_linetype.
  • Layout and labels: xlim, ylim, coord_flip, coord_cartesian, facet_wrap, facet_grid, labs, ggtitle, xlab, and ylab.
  • Themes and size: theme, the theme_* presets, and size.
  • Interaction: tooltip, interactive, brush, and highlight.
  • Statistics: coefplot, residual_plot, qq_plot, fitted_plot, scale_location_plot, diagnostic_plots, effect_plot, predictions, marginal_means, random_effects_plot, and interval_plot.
