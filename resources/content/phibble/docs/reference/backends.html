->phibble()

$builder->phibble(): SqlTable

A lazy SqlTable over a Laravel query, Eloquent, or relation builder, on the builder’s connection.

Global scopes, such as soft deletes, apply. Attribute casts and accessors do not, because rows come from SQL.

On Illuminate, Illuminate, and Illuminate, after PhibbleServiceProvider::registerMacros(), which package discovery runs.

->toDataFrame()

$builderOrCollection->toDataFrame(array $types = []): DataFrame

Run a Laravel query, or read a collection, into a DataFrame.

On builders it calls DataFrame::fromQuery(); on Collection and LazyCollection it calls DataFrame::fromCollection(). $types declares column types, as in fromColumns().

On the query, Eloquent, and relation builders, Illuminate, and Illuminate, after PhibbleServiceProvider::registerMacros(), which package discovery runs.
